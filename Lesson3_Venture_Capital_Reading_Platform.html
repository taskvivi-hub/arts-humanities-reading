Paragraph 1 · Introduction

1. Venture capital (VC) companies are firms that fund startups or small businesses for long-term growth.

2. They can also provide managerial expertise or other resources.

3. They bring in wealthy investors and find investment bankers and other financial institutions to pool for an investment target.

4. Other than return on investment (ROI) upon exiting, usually they will also take equity in the firm that they invest in order to have a say in decision making.

創業投資（VC）公司是為新創企業或小型企業提供資金，以支持其長期成長的公司。

這些創投公司也可以提供管理方面的專業知識與技能，或其他資源。

他們會引進資金雄厚的投資人，並尋找投資銀行家及其他金融機構，共同籌集資金以投資特定的目標企業。

除了在退出投資時獲得投資報酬之外，他們通常也會取得所投資公司的股權，以便在公司的決策過程中擁有發言權。

如今，矽谷仍然是科技創新的中心，這主要是因為舊金山灣區的新創企業數量持續增加。

Paragraph 3 · Investment Activity

1. Undoubtedly, the Silicon Valley tech hub is the 800 lb. gorilla when it comes to both venture-backed tech financing and exit activity.

2. Since the start of 2009, venture capital firms have deployed $31.5 billion across 3,308 deals into Silicon Valley-based tech startups.

3. In fact, Silicon Valley has consistently taken over 40% of venture capital deals and over 50% of funding to tech startups across seven major U.S. venture hubs including New York and Massachusetts.

毫無疑問，無論是在創投支持的科技企業融資，或是投資退出活動方面，矽谷這個科技中心都是具有壓倒性影響力的主導者。

自 2009 年初以來，創投公司已透過 3,308 筆投資交易，向總部位於矽谷的科技新創企業投入了 315 億美元。

事實上，在包括紐約和麻薩諸塞州在內的美國七大創投中心之中，矽谷一直占有超過 40% 的創投交易數量，以及超過 50% 的科技新創企業投資資金。

Paragraph 4 · Exit Strategies

1. Every investment has a risk and having a good exit strategy is the most important factor that venture capitalists look at before any investment.

2. Silicon Valley companies make up over 1/2 of the top 50 largest venture-backed tech exits and the lion’s share of value as measured by exit valuation since 2012.

3. Clearly, Silicon Valley continues to dominate the attention of hungry tech entrepreneurs striving to build the next big thing and the venture capital tech investors hoping to back them.

每一項投資都有風險，而擁有良好的退出策略，是創業投資人在進行任何投資之前最重視的因素。

自 2012 年以來，在規模最大的前 50 筆創投支持科技企業退出交易中，矽谷公司占了超過一半，而且以退出時的企業估值來衡量，矽谷公司也占據了其中絕大部分的價值。

顯然，矽谷持續吸引著雄心勃勃、努力打造下一個突破性產品或企業的科技創業家，以及希望提供資金支持這些創業家的科技創投投資人。

Paragraph 5 · Funding Trends

1. Silicon Valley venture capital investments in tech continue to grow.

2. Since the start of 2009, Silicon Valley tech startups have raised over $31 billion across 3,308 deals.

3. And VC financing is not slowing down anytime soon.

4. 2013 is on pace for a five-year high in venture capital and funding activity, as the first three quarters of 2013 saw nearly 5% more dollars and 8% more deals as compared to the first three quarters of 2012.

5. The lion’s share of deal activity in Silicon Valley tech has gone to the Internet sector.

6. Specifically, the Internet sector claimed 1/2 of Silicon Valley deal share in 2012 and 2013 year-to-date.

矽谷對科技產業的創業投資持續成長。

自 2009 年初以來，矽谷科技新創企業已透過 3,308 筆投資交易，募集超過 310 億美元的資金。

而且，創投融資活動短期內並沒有放緩的跡象。

2013 年的創投及融資活動正朝著五年來最高水準邁進，因為與 2012 年前三季相比，2013 年前三季的投資金額增加了將近 5%，交易筆數則增加了 8%。

矽谷科技產業的投資交易活動中，絕大部分都集中在網際網路產業。

具體而言，在 2012 年及 2013 年截至當時的統計期間內，網際網路產業占了矽谷投資交易總數的一半。

Paragraph 6 · Successful Investments

1. Knowing which startup has high potential is not a gamble, but sometimes it does take a lot of gut feelings.

2. Sequoia Capital is by far the most successful VC.

3. Their most famous deal was their investment at Google at its initial stage.

4. Successful investment may not come easy, but once it happens, VCs can reap a lot from the deal and generate more sales opportunities in the future as a result of its success history.

判斷哪一家新創企業具有高度發展潛力並不是單純的賭博，但有時確實需要依靠大量的直覺判斷。

紅杉資本（Sequoia Capital）是目前為止最成功的創投公司。

他們最著名的一筆投資交易，就是在 Google 創業初期對其進行投資。

成功的投資可能得來不易，但一旦投資成功，創投公司就能從交易中獲得豐厚的回報，並憑藉過去成功投資的紀錄，在未來創造更多業務機會。
